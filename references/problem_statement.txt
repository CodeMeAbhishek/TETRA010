Track A: HealthTech 
Problem Statement 1 
AI-Powered Lifestyle Disease Risk Prediction & Early Referral Assistant 
 
Background 
India is witnessing a rapid rise in lifestyle diseases such as diabetes, hypertension, chronic 
kidney disease (CKD), cardiovascular disease, and stroke. Despite the availability of effective 
screening and treatment protocols, many patients, especially in rural and semi-urban regions 
are diagnosed only after developing irreversible complications. 
Primary care providers often face challenges such as limited access to updated clinical 
guidelines, time constraints, incomplete patient records, and lack of decision-support tools. 
Preventive risk assessment, regular monitoring, and timely referrals remain inconsistent, 
resulting in avoidable morbidity, mortality, and healthcare costs. 
Problem Statement 
Design an AI-powered clinical decision support assistant that enables early identification of 
individuals at high risk for lifestyle diseases and their complications. 
The solution should utilize readily available patient information including demographics, 
symptoms, vital signs, medical history, medications, and laboratory investigations—to 
generate personalized risk assessments. It should identify missing investigations, 
recommend evidence-based screening tests, detect warning signs for disease progression, 
and advise timely referral to qualified healthcare professionals whenever appropriate. 
The solution should be intuitive, affordable, multilingual, and capable of functioning 
independently of any specific hospital information system or healthcare provider. It should 
also provide explainable AI outputs so that clinicians understand the rationale behind 
recommendations. 
Expected Outcomes 
• Risk Scoring: AI-generated risk scores for diabetes, hypertension, CKD, 
cardiovascular disease, and stroke. 
• Early Warning: Early warning alerts for patients likely to develop complications. 
• Clinical Guidance: Recommendations for missing investigations based on standard 
clinical guidelines. 
• Referral Protocols: Referral suggestions based on patient risk profile. 
• Patient Engagement: Patient-friendly education material in regional languages. 
• Scalable Prototype: A scalable prototype suitable for deployment in resource-limited 
primary healthcare settings.
